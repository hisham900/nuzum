About Oxygen Oasis

Oxygen Oasis builds management systems for companies and shops in Oman. Its point-of-sale product, Oxygen Oasis Retail (previously named Nuzum), is a point-of-sale and accounting program in Arabic and English that runs on the shop's own computer and works without internet. The shop's data stays on the shop's own computer. Optional add-ons keep a copy of the daily backup in the shop owner's own Google Drive, and put a sales summary in the owner's own Google account so they can read it on their phone. We have no server of our own that stores shop or Google data.
